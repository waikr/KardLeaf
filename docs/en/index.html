Why KardLeaf

Lightweight, not limited

卡叶笔记 KardLeaf is built around real Markdown / TXT files, keeping your notes open, portable, and easy to use with tools such as Obsidian.

01

Local files first

Your content is stored as real Markdown / TXT files, making backup, migration, sync, and cross-app editing straightforward.

notes/├─ Ideas.md└─ Projects/Plan.md
02

Live editing and preview

Work with common Markdown formatting, tasks, images, code blocks, and formulas, then switch quickly between writing and previewing.

03

Flexible organization

Multi-level folders, categories, sidebar navigation, tags, and properties help keep both quick notes and large libraries organized.

04

Fits existing workflows

Use the same files with Obsidian, VS Code, Typora, WebDAV, or Syncthing without moving into a closed format.

05

Your data, your choice

No forced account and no forced cloud upload. You decide where notes live and how they are synchronized.

Interface preview

Familiar, clear, focused

From quick capture to long-form writing, the interface stays centered on your content.
